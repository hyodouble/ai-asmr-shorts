3. Tofu "helper" stories still outperform Mochi on average, but Mochi helper episodes (#24, #34, #36) are now Mochi's best.

6. Naver blog is the only track with a clear upward trend (9 → 27 → 74 views/day).

8. Consider dropping YouTube upload effort or changing the YouTube format (titles/hook) since it costs time for ~0 return.
